Restore a personal digital identity with two independent approvals and a device-secured factor.

01Create identity
?

The browser creates an identity key and encrypts it locally.

02Protect with 2-of-3
?

The recovery secret is divided between three independent authorities. Any two shares can reconstruct it.

03Lose device
?

The device can disappear without giving any authority enough information to restore the identity alone.

04Approve recovery
?

Two selected authorities independently verify the request and release their shares.

05Restore identity
?

The two shares combine with the device-secured factor to decrypt and restore the identity key.
