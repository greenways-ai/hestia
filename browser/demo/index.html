Each authority runs its own Hara kernel ?

Every authority owns its policy, evidence decision, chat state, and share-release decision. It cannot inspect another authority's kernel state.

This browser demo places the independent kernels together only to make the mechanism visible.

02Split 2 of 3
?

Hara divides the recovery secret among three independent authorities. Any two shares can reconstruct it.

03Lose
?

The device is gone. No authority holds the complete recovery secret or the personal factor.

04Approve
?

Two chosen authorities independently verify the request and release their shares.

05Restore
?

The two shares combine with the personal factor to decrypt and restore the identity key.
