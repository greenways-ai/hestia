02 · Give the work a shape

Issue a signed mandate.

A mandate says who may do the work, what the brief is, which workflow applies and where human approval is still required.

Signed brief

Place the source material in the office.

Hestia signs a versioned document root. The agent receives the approved brief without gaining authority to rewrite the original.

Private update

Receive a signed, encrypted progress note.

The specialist signs the encrypted envelope. Hestia records the commitment and workflow intent while the plaintext stays inside the current office epoch.

Workflow entry

Record what the agent actually did.

A work entry binds a named step, the action taken and the resulting artefact to the active mandate. This is the basis of a later receipt.

03 · Decisions stay human

Let the agent prepare terms. Keep the final word.

The agent can propose and revise a precise offer. Hestia accepts only the exact signed root that you approve.

04 · Private record, presentable on demand

Close the mandate with a receipt.
